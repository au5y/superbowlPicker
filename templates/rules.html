How to Play

1 Making Predictions
Answer as many prop bets as you can before kickoff!

Use the My Picks page to select your answers. Questions range from the Coin Toss result to Golden Gate Bridge sightings, and maybe even some football.

🔒 Once the game starts, the pool will LOCK and no further edits can be made.
2 Scoring
Every correct prediction earns you 1 Point.

The leaderboard updates live as questions are resolved during the Super Bowl. Check the Leaderboard to track your standing against your group and the global pool.
3 The Tie Breaker
In the event of a tie in Total Points at the end of the game, the winner is decided by the Scoreboard Prediction.

Step 1: Closest to Total The winner is the player whose predicted total score (Home + Away) is mathematically closest to the actual final score.
Example: If Final Score is 50. Guess of 48 (Diff 2) beats Guess of 53 (Diff 3).
Step 2: Closest to Exact Score If still tied, the winner is the player with the lowest absolute difference between their predicted team scores and the actual team scores.
Final: SEA 25 - NE 24 (Total 49)
Player A picks 26-24 (Total 50). Diff: |26-25| + |24-24| = 1
Player B picks 40-10 (Total 50). Diff: |40-25| + |10-24| = 29
Player A wins.
4 Groups
You can switch the leaderboard to see any group you are in.

Check your Profile to join specific groups using a Room Code.
